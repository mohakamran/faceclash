      What do the numbers mean?
      • Everyone starts at 0. Scores are saved, so they carry on between visits.
      • Score: winning a matchup adds points and losing takes points away, so a score can go below 0.
      • Each vote moves up to 32 points. Beating someone with a higher score earns more. Beating someone lower earns less. (This is the Elo system used in chess.)
      • Wins: matchups won, e.g. 7 of 10. Click any face to see its history.
      • Tiers: 🆕 New · ❄️ Cold (below −20) · ☀️ Warming up · 🌶️ Hot (20+) · 🔥 On fire (50+) · 👑 Legend (100+). 🔥3 means 3 wins in a row.
